Research

Expanding what frontier agents can do.

I build benchmarks, RL environments and independent evaluation pipelines for frontier agents, so they can expand their capabilities into open problems and new domains they have not yet mastered.

01

New domains & open problems

Expanding frontier agents into domains they cannot handle yet: computer-use agents, mobile-use agents, embodied agents, agents for scientific research, and more. Each domain becomes tasks with verifiers and an independent evaluation.

FrontierPro LiveWorldBench CUA-SWE CatchBench AgentSocialBench WeClawArena

02

Post-training & agentic RL

RL environments where the verifier is the reward, and post-training recipes for multi-turn, long-horizon agents that still hold up when they move from simulation to the real world.

SLEA-RL When Simulation Lies

03

Recursive self-improvement

Environments and loops in which agents evaluate, train and improve themselves round after round, from memory that evolves without training to repeated cycles of evaluation and RL.

Agent Frontier

Founder & organizer

Taking frontier agents where they have not been.

Agent Frontier is a multi-university research group dedicated to bringing frontier agents into open domains and problems they have not yet mastered. For each new domain we build AI-native environments, benchmarks and RL environments, run independent evaluations of frontier agents, and post-train them there, round after round, toward recursive self-improvement.

With researchers from CMU, USC, UCLA, Arizona State, UW–Madison and Princeton.
